# What makes a board screen good

Use these when you rate board screenshots yourself, with a colleague, or with a scoring tool of your own.
Screenshot sizes: phone 390 × 844, laptop 1280 × 800.

## Five criteria

| Criterion | Passes when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| clear | The reader sees within 5 seconds what the section is and what it is for | Vague, confusing, needs a second read |
| act | The intended next step is clear and easy | No action stands out, or it takes effort |
| honest | Only plainly true facts; examples are labelled; unfinished things are marked | Made-up stats, promises |
| copy | Short, concrete, natural words, no filler | Wordy, generic, stiff, translated-sounding |
| fit | Content and tone suit the reader and the job | Wrong content or tone for the reader |

## Checks

- **Everyday words**, no buzzwords. Longest sentence: at most 16 words.
- **No invented numbers.** Counts must be real data of this page (the board's own AC counts are fine).
- **Measured, not guessed.** Check layout facts: button height at least 44px on a phone, what is in view on the
  first screen, nothing floating over text.
- **One clear main action** per screen. A row of equal buttons scores low.
- **Main thing first.** What the reader opened the page for comes first (results, state). Explanations come after it.
- **State on top, then detail**: title, state and count first, then each item in its own words. Codes or
  abbreviations in place of words score low.

## Applied to tuned.board

- Mapa AC: each AC row says its state in words and marks (✓ ▶ ○ –), not colour only, so readers see which ACs are
  finished without expanding cards (clear, act).
- Counts in Stan tablicy, real counts only: "Dev checklist skończony: X z N AC", "Podpisy: DEV x/N …" (honest).
- One next step per AC: "Czeka na: …" names the roles. The DEV hint says which button to click (act).
- Short lines, at most 16 words per sentence (copy).
- Phone 390px: no sideways scroll; buttons at least 44px tall.
